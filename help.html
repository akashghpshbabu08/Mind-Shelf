Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I create and organize notes?

Go to My Notes and click Create a note. You can organize by subject, tags, or add them to My Collections.

How does sharing resources work?

In the Resource Library, you can share useful links and documents with your college peers. Materials are curated by community members.

What is Shared Knowledge?

Shared Knowledge is our community discussion space where students can post questions, explanations, and key takeaways.
