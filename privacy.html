LEGAL & POLICIES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when registering for an account (name, college email, password hash) and study content you store or share on the platform.

2. How We Protect Your Data

Passwords are securely hashed using bcrypt before storage. Communication with the backend is encrypted, and your session tokens are stored securely in your browser's session storage.

3. Data Sharing

MindShelf does not sell your personal information. Only content you explicitly mark or publish to community areas (such as Shared Resources or Shared Knowledge) is visible to other college members.
